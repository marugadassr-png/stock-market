Portfolio fundamentals
The five principles this dashboard is built on.
1. Define your goals first+

Retirement, a house, passive income, or just building wealth — the goal decides the strategy, not the other way around.

2. Match risk to time horizon+

A 25+ year runway can absorb crypto-level volatility. That's the entire justification for a 65% crypto sleeve at 29 — it would not hold for a 5-year goal.

3. Diversify inside crypto too+

"Crypto" isn't one asset class. Core (BTC/ETH), infra L1s, and degen plays carry very different risk — size them accordingly.

4. DCA beats timing+

Buying a fixed dollar amount on a schedule smooths entry price and removes the temptation to time tops and bottoms.

5. Rebalance on drift, not vibes+

When a sleeve drifts more than ~5 points from target after a rally, trim back to plan. It forces selling strength systematically.
